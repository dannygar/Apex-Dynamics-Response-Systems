# Method 2 — Research Plan (retrospective: corpus already collected)

Coach note: method-sequencing allows skipping execution "when the user demonstrates completed prior work". Docs D01–D11 are treated as the completed corpus; this plan reconstructs what the research *was* and states plainly what it was *not*.

## Objectives (reconstructed from D01 "Unresolved at close")

1. Identify the primary user(s) and their first question under load.
2. Understand how uncertainty, freshness, and disagreement are experienced per role.
3. Understand how action moves from decision to field (and back).
4. Surface whose needs are invisible to a command-center view.
5. Understand the funding/demonstration constraint.

## Known methodological limits

* Single interviewer (Kadijah) across all seven interviews — single-lens risk.
* Replay sample: "a conversation aid, not a representative data set" (D02, Kadijah); cannot demonstrate live reliability or universal thresholds (D05).
* No quantitative baselines (time-to-picture, stale-assignment rate, missed-correction count).
* No field-tested data of any kind; no user has touched an artifact.

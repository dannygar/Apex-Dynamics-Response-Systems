# Method 1 — Scope Boundaries (conversational, not a requirements doc)

## Original request (verbatim, D01 Background)

> Apex Dynamics Response Systems was asked to create a unified experience that brings all available data together visually, helping regional response centers make the best possible decisions and protect the largest number of lives.

## Frozen vs. fluid classification

**Classification: FROZEN surface, FLUID underneath.**

* Frozen signals: names a specific form ("unified experience", "visually") and a specific context ("regional response centers"); the phrase "unified operating picture" arrived pre-formed from three centers (D01 00:00).
* Fluid signals: the participants themselves doubt it is one experience — "Not for me" (Sabien, D01 05:14); "Both, probably" on whether it is an operating-model project (Frans, D01 03:37); Kadijah frames it as "a starting hypothesis, not a validated answer" (D01 07:02).
* Coach rule applied: "When uncertain, classify as frozen and explore the driving problem" (method-01-scope).

## Discovered problem (draft, Method 1 version)

`[SIMULATED TEAM]` after coach pushback to remove the embedded "one shared view" solution:

> People in different response roles cannot quickly build — and keep — a trustworthy, shared understanding of an evolving kaiju incident: what is known, how sure we are, what changed, who knows it, and what is actually in motion. The gaps are uneven, so the places and people with the least signal are the easiest to miss.

Gap vs. original request: the request is about *bringing data together visually*; the discovered problem is about *meaning, timing, acknowledgment, and coverage* — data is already together enough to drown in (D01 Background, D02).

## In scope (fluid — open to change)

* Shared situational understanding across command, dispatch, data ops, field (D01).
* Evidence provenance, disagreement, freshness, and silence (D02, D03, D05).
* Change awareness and handoff continuity (D01 03:02, D04).
* Field corrections flowing back into the regional understanding (D02, D06).
* Coverage gaps by community (D07) and access needs (D08).

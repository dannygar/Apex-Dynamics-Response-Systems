# Method 3 — Problem Definition

## Original request (D01)

"Create a unified experience that brings all available data together visually."

## Discovered problem

Regional kaiju response depends on several roles — command, dispatch, data operations, and field units — acting on a shared understanding that no one currently holds. The data is already together enough to drown in; what is missing is **meaning that survives speed**: which kind of "current" each fact is, what silence means, whether a commitment is real, whether a summary is still contested, what changed and who knows it, and where weak observation is hiding people at risk. Today that understanding is rebuilt by hand, held in individual heads, lost at handoff, and biased toward whoever generates the most signal.

## Scope

* In: shared incident understanding across roles; evidence meaning (provenance, freshness, disagreement, silence); commitment and acknowledgment states; field-to-region corrections; coverage gaps; orientation vs inspection contexts.
* Out (this phase): automated decision-making or dispatch; live integrations; claims of production accuracy.

## Stakeholders (by consequence)

Command, dispatch, data ops, field units (primary); communities with weak signal and people needing more time or different transport (high consequence, no direct voice); sponsor and steering group (funding).

## Constraints that shape any answer

June steering review (~12 weeks); possibly one artifact; replay-only data; no validated threat rule; radio/paper fallback must remain; no added documentation burden; accessibility (non-color, keyboard, magnification, reduced motion).

## Success signals (from participants, not invented)

* Command spends "less time asking everyone to repeat the current picture and more time deciding what risk we are willing to take" (D03).
* "Fewer assignments based on conditions that stopped being true" (D06).
* "Silence prompts a question instead of reassurance, and response priorities reflect consequence rather than data volume" (D07).
* People "notice the right change under load" (D04).
* The test "does not exclude people... by construction" (D08).

## Known weaknesses in this definition

Community and accessibility perspectives each rest on one intermediary interview; no observation; no baselines; team alignment simulated, not confirmed.

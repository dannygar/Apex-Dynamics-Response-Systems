# Method 3 — Insight Statements

Format: insight → why it matters → design implication (direction, not solution) → sources → confidence.

1. **Time words carry four different meanings, and roles silently assume different ones.** (T1)
   Why: a correct fact read as the wrong kind of "current" drives the wrong action (buses staged while command still debated, D01 01:11).
   Implication: whatever is built must make the *kind* of currency legible per item and per role.
   Sources: D01, D02, D05, D11 · Confidence: High

2. **The most dangerous situations can look the quietest.** (T2, T7)
   Why: interference and damaged infrastructure remove evidence exactly where risk and isolation are highest (D05, D07).
   Implication: absence of signal must be treated as a question with an owner, not as reassurance.
   Sources: D01, D02, D05, D07, D10 · Confidence: High (mechanism) / Medium (community impact)

3. **Commitment is a negotiated multi-step loop, but systems record it as a single state change.** (T3, T5)
   Why: "covered" at handoff turned out to be unacknowledged and route-blocked (D04); a button makes action "look immediate" (D06).
   Implication: the gap between intent, acknowledgment, and readiness has to stay visible to whoever relies on it.
   Sources: D02, D04, D06, D11 · Confidence: High

4. **A shared shorthand is valuable precisely because it is fast — which is also why it anchors people prematurely.** (T4)
   Why: "condition four" coordinates a crowded channel (D03) yet "sounds settled when the evidence behind it is not".
   Implication: summaries must stay challengeable and traceable to who confirmed them; disagreement must survive the summary.
   Sources: D01, D03, D05, D07, D10 · Confidence: High

5. **The regional picture is only as good as its slowest correction path.** (T5)
   Why: field truth (water on the approach, a blocked bridge) took minutes and three hops to reach command, and may never have reached the route system (D01, D06).
   Implication: the field is a source, not just a recipient — without adding work during movement (T10).
   Sources: D01, D02, D06, D10 · Confidence: High (single field voice)

6. **Expertise in the head evaporates at handoff.** (T6)
   Why: mental filters and "who knows what" are not recorded anywhere (D03, D04).
   Implication: change and acknowledgment history matter as much as the current state.
   Sources: D01, D02, D03, D04 · Confidence: High

7. **"Visual" smuggles in an untested assumption about who, where, and how people use the information.** (T8)
   Why: room-scale orientation and desk-scale inspection fail in opposite ways (D08); color-only meaning excludes users.
   Implication: separate the jobs of orientation and inspection before choosing any layout.
   Sources: D01, D03, D08, D09 · Confidence: Medium–High

8. **The artifact that wins funding can teach everyone the wrong mental model.** (T9)
   Why: a smooth replay "looks like a live operational system" (D10); viewers learn from the demo, not the caveats (D11).
   Implication: what the artifact is for (persuade vs. learn) must be decided and labeled explicitly.
   Sources: D01, D08, D09, D10, D11 · Confidence: High (conflicting priorities)
